The game is not about solving puzzles but about finding out how to solve them. What starts with stablishing ways to communicate ends up as total understanding without words needed, with the game embracing this in its final moments. 

What starts with everyone getting out of a tunnel and finding everyone, connecting with them and then going through the game together ends by having to say goodbye to them before they go to the light at the end of the tunnel. Even if it wasn't a metaphor of life itself, it's still such a beautiful way to close the adventure. Probably one of the best endings in any videogame.
